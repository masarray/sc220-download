# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SC220 Live aims to maintain a professional, technically useful, and welcoming public repository. Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, provide factual information, and help keep discussions focused on reproducible product, documentation, website, and release-distribution topics.

Positive participation includes:

- Being respectful toward people with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Describing technical problems clearly and patiently.
- Giving constructive feedback about code, documentation, design, or behavior rather than attacking individuals.
- Correcting mistakes openly and accepting reasonable technical review.
- Protecting private information, credentials, activation data, and security-sensitive details.
- Respecting proprietary source boundaries and third-party intellectual property.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Posting credentials, license data, signing material, malware, exploit details, or sensitive logs publicly.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, fabricated reviews, manipulated benchmarks, or knowingly false product claims.
- Repeatedly demanding private source code, proprietary DSP implementation details, or confidential infrastructure after scope has been explained.
- Distributing unauthorized or repackaged application binaries through this repository.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, documentation contributions, and other official SC220 Live community spaces managed by the maintainer.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Do not escalate sensitive conduct or security matters through a detailed public issue. Open a minimal issue requesting private contact, or use GitHub's private security-reporting path when the matter involves a vulnerability.

Include only the information needed to identify the incident. Do not repost harmful content unnecessarily.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior is inconsistent with this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider severity, context, repetition, technical impact, and the safety of repository participants.

Good-faith disagreement and critical technical feedback are welcome when expressed professionally and supported by evidence.
