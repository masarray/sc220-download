SIMPLE SETUP

From download to broadcast-ready in four steps.

The installer can offer Standard VB-Audio VB-CABLE, which carries the final mix from SC220 Live into your streaming software.

Download installer →
  1. 01

    Install SC220 Live

    Run the installer as Administrator. Select VB-CABLE when it is not already installed.

  2. 02

    Select your audio sources

    Choose Recording Tech SC220 as the main input and the correct Windows playback device for PC Audio.

  3. 03

    Select Stream Output

    Choose CABLE Input in SC220 Live as the destination for the final mix.

  4. 04

    Connect your streaming app

    In OBS or TikTok Live Studio, add CABLE Output as an audio source.

Before pressing GO LIVE

Select every device, test your complete path, and make sure the meters are not constantly reaching the red zone.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers.

Can I try SC220 Live immediately?

Yes. Every creative control is available for 365 days without a credit card. Core audio functions continue to work afterward.

Do I have to use OBS?

No. Any application that can receive a Windows audio device may work, including TikTok Live Studio.

What does VB-CABLE do?

VB-CABLE carries the final mix from SC220 Live to your streaming application. Select CABLE Input in SC220 Live and CABLE Output in the destination app.

Does the application require a permanent internet connection?

No for normal audio processing. Internet is needed for downloading and may be needed for optional activation.

Why does PC audio lower while I speak?

That is automatic ducking. It helps your voice stay clear over music or other computer audio.
