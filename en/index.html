What does a SmartScreen warning mean?

SmartScreen uses app and publisher reputation as one of its signals. Newer or less widely downloaded software can therefore show a warning. The warning by itself does not prove a file is malicious or safe, which is why the official source and file verification matter.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Short answers, no runaround.

Is SC220 Live really free to try?

Yes. Full control is available for 365 days without a credit card or automatic billing. After that, core audio functions remain available while selected creative controls become read-only.

Why can Windows SmartScreen appear?

SmartScreen uses app and publisher reputation as one of its signals. Newer or less widely downloaded software can show a warning. Use the official source and compare the published SHA-256 if you want to verify the file.

Is SC220 Live karaoke software?

SC220 Live is an audio mixer and live-streaming console. It does not manage a song catalogue or display karaoke lyrics.

Do I have to use OBS?

No. Other Windows applications that accept audio endpoints can also be used.

What does VB-CABLE do?

VB-CABLE carries the final mix from SC220 Live into your broadcasting application.
